Frequently Answered Queries

Technical answers to operational and architectural questions.

Is my clinical health data secured under legal regulatory criteria?

Yes. All Patient Health Information (PHI) processes utilize enterprise-grade AES-256 data encryption rules at rest, with secure TLS 1.3 encryption standard pipelines enforcing privacy protection on all active network transits.

How does the system eliminate calendar double-bookings?

The scheduling backend executes strict multi-concurrency transactional verification models. When an appointment block is selected, the database sets exclusive transactional locks to prevent concurrent booking claims on that specific time slice.
